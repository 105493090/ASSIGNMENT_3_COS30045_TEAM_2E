Risk & Longevity

Exploring health risk factors across OECD countries. This project examines how smoking rates and obesity levels have changed over time across 15 nations, using data from the OECD Health Statistics database.

Smoking

How do daily smoking rates compare across countries? Which nations have seen the biggest decline?

Obesity

How prevalent is obesity across OECD nations? How does it differ between males and females?

About the data

The data used in this project comes from the OECD Risk Factors for Health dataset, covering 15 countries from 2015 to 2025. It includes measures such as daily smoker rates, obesity and overweight percentages, and tobacco consumption, broken down by sex.
