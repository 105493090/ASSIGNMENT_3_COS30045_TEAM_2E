Risk & Longevity

Exploring health risk factors and health outcomes across OECD countries. This project examines how smoking rates, obesity levels, and life expectancy compare across 15 nations, using data from the OECD Health Statistics database.

Smoking

How do daily smoking rates compare across countries? Which nations have seen the biggest decline?

Obesity

How prevalent is obesity across OECD nations? How does it differ between males and females?

Life Expectancy

How long do people live across OECD countries? How does this relate to health risk factors?

About the data

This project uses two datasets from the OECD. The first covers risk factors for health, including daily smoker rates, obesity and overweight percentages, and tobacco consumption. The second covers health status, including life expectancy at birth. Both datasets cover 15 countries from 2015 to 2025, broken down by sex.
